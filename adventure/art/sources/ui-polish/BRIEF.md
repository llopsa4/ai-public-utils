## Work-gloved interaction cursor

A worn ochre-brown leather work glove pointing toward the upper-left: one clearly extended index finger, curled remaining fingers, a separate thumb and a short cuff. Dark near-black outline, restrained warm highlights and broad readable pixels. No extra fingers, gauntlet machinery, lettering, scenery or baked drop shadow. Transparent background. Deliver a 32×32 PNG; fingertip is the click hotspot, verified after reduction. Keep eye, speech and exit cursors.
