## Description

His mother's Courier Gauntlet: an oversized pressure gauntlet for the right forearm and hand, a clenched fist of massive cream-ceramic knuckles, thick brass reinforcements, a broad cuff, a pressure pipe along the forearm, a round ember-cell housing on the back of the hand. Tier 1, the humble original: worn, patched, mismatched parts, dull metal and chipped ceramic.

## Use and placement

A gear picture on the gear screen (the paper doll's slot and the loot-style preview). Tier 1 of 10: keep the same silhouette, framing and angle as the other tiers of this piece.
