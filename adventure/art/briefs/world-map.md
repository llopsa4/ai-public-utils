## Description

A coloured engraved atlas (Codex, 2026-11-10, chosen over moonlit and grey-engraved versions: warm brown land, Ironhaven on a snowy mountain, a blue river, dark forests, copper roofs; same places), of an invented world map: one continent split north to south by the Rift, a deep jagged chasm crossed by a single bridge (Spanwick) at its middle; west of it the grey ash plains around Cinderfold, low foundry hills, and a snowy north with a walled town (Ironhaven); east of it a river with waterwheels (Alderweir), a forest in the south-east, a large old hearth-works further east (the First Hearth), and mountains in the north-east with a lone hut. A dotted road runs from Cinderfold east over the bridge. Follow the layout image exactly: every place sits where its mark is. No words, letters or labels anywhere (the game writes them).

## Use and placement

The world map in the notebook's Map tab, shown about 480 px wide. The game writes each known place's name next to its mark and pins where the character is, so the painting has no lettering at all.
