## Description

Bryn, the gauntlet smith of the tempering house, in her thirties: nearly as tall as Mara and much broader, heavily muscled shoulders and forearms from hauling crucibles. Deep brown skin, black hair in thick braids tied back under a dark red kerchief, a scar through her left eyebrow, a quick wide grin. A sleeveless dark-red work shirt, a thick singed leather apron, leather wraps on both forearms, heavy gloves tucked in her belt and a pair of long tongs hanging from it. Canvas trousers, scuffed steel-toed boots.
