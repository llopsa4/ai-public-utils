## Description

Mara, the courier (he/him), about 22: the tallest of the cast but lean and long-limbed, a runner's build. Warm brown skin, short dark-brown hair swept back and a little unruly, a light stubble, hazel eyes, a determined, slightly tired face. An ochre wool scarf wrapped twice round his neck, its long end thrown over his left shoulder like a short cloak. Cream-white fired-ceramic armour plates (chest, shoulders, forearms, shins), chipped and patched, laced over dark brown leather. A worn leather satchel on his left hip. Dark work trousers with ceramic knee plates, patched at the knees; brown leather courier boots with brass buckles. His mother's pressure gauntlet on his right forearm and hand, oversized: a clenched fist of massive cream-ceramic knuckles, thick riveted brass reinforcements, a broad cuff and a pressure pipe along the forearm, the fist reaching down towards his knee; a round ember-cell housing on the back of the hand (unlit: dull dark red glass). Keep to Codex's pilot design (art/inbox/reference/gauntlet-closed-v2/mara-master.png). In his medium armour (exactly the outfit of this pack's right-facing master.png), now drawn FACING LEFT. The left-facing walk master: standing in profile facing LEFT, in the neutral stance the walk cycle starts from (feet slightly apart, both visible, arms hanging relaxed and a little away from the body). Only one eye shows (profile). The same rules as the light armour, in the medium (layered ceramic) armour of this pack's master.png: the gauntlet on his RIGHT arm is on the FAR side, behind his body; the scarf end over his LEFT shoulder and the satchel on his LEFT hip are on the NEAR side (the satchel part of the torso layer, above mid-thigh).
