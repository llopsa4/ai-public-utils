## Description

Mara, the courier (he/him), about 22: the tallest of the cast but lean and long-limbed, a runner's build. Warm brown skin, short dark-brown hair swept back and a little unruly, a light stubble, hazel eyes, a determined, slightly tired face. An ochre wool scarf wrapped twice round his neck, its long end thrown over his left shoulder like a short cloak. Cream-white fired-ceramic armour plates (chest, shoulders, forearms, shins), chipped and patched, laced over dark brown leather. A worn leather satchel on his left hip. Dark work trousers with ceramic knee plates, patched at the knees; brown leather courier boots with brass buckles. His mother's pressure gauntlet on his right forearm and hand, oversized: a clenched fist of massive cream-ceramic knuckles, thick riveted brass reinforcements, a broad cuff and a pressure pipe along the forearm, the fist reaching down towards his knee; a round ember-cell housing on the back of the hand (unlit: dull dark red glass). Keep to Codex's pilot design (art/inbox/reference/gauntlet-closed-v2/mara-master.png). In his light armour (exactly the outfit of this pack's right-facing master.png), now drawn FACING LEFT. The joint map of left/master.png: a small solid dot of a fixed colour at the centre of each joint (near and far shoulder, elbow, hip, knee and ankle), near and far as seen facing left. His mother's pressure gauntlet is on his RIGHT forearm and hand, so facing left it is on the FAR side: behind his body, the big ceramic fist partly hidden by his torso and hips, swinging behind him as he walks (in the walk it belongs to the far forearm layer). His near (left) forearm is his own: the ceramic forearm plate over leather, a bare hand. The ochre scarf's long end, thrown over his LEFT shoulder, is now on the NEAR side, hanging down over his near shoulder and back; the satchel on his LEFT hip is on the NEAR side, in front of the near hip (part of the torso layer: keep it above mid-thigh so the near leg can swing under it). The ember cell glass on the back of the gauntlet's hand faces away from us on this side: show it only where it would truly be seen.
