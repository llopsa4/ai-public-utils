## Description

Captain Sigrid of Ironhaven (she/her), about 35, a main character: tall (nearly Mara's height) and fit, an athletic soldier's build like Mara's but a woman's: strong shoulders and arms, a lean waist, powerful legs, upright and balanced like a fencer. A dark-blue winter officer's coat with brass buttons and a short snowy fur collar, with chipped, repaired ivory ceramic protection worked into it (shoulder, chest and ribs, forearms). Her artifact is Nessa's mechanically extendable sword: while she walks or stands only its compact hilt shows, clipped to her belt at the rear hip, with no blade and no scabbard (leather grip, ceramic pommel, a folded brass heat-sink guard, a ceramic chamber with a copper coil, an activation button, a flush emitter collar); at the press of the button physical blade sections extend and lock together, with ceramic channels and copper joints (a mechanical blade, never an energy blade).
