## Description

Captain Sigrid of Ironhaven (she/her), about 35, a main character: tall (nearly Mara's height) and fit, an athletic soldier's build like Mara's but a woman's: strong shoulders and arms, a lean waist, powerful legs, upright and balanced like a fencer.
