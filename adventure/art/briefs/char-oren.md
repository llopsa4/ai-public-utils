## Description

Oren, the old regulator keeper, about 68: tall (nearly Mara's height) but a little stooped, thin. Pale weathered skin with ruddy cheeks, white hair tied back in a short tail, a neat white beard, round brass spectacles on his nose, bushy white brows. An iron-grey waistcoat over a collarless off-white shirt, sleeve garters, a long brown leather apron with tools in its pockets, a brass pressure gauge on a chain at his belt and a small brass gauge held in his left hand. Grey trousers, black boots.
