## Description

Mara off duty: the same face, hair, skin and build as Mara (identical proportions and height), but no armour, no gauntlet and no satchel. A plain undyed linen shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, a faded rust-red knitted waistcoat, the ochre scarf hanging loose around his neck, dark work trousers (no knee plates), soft brown boots. Both hands bare; a thin leather cord bracelet on the right wrist where the gauntlet usually sits.

## Use and placement

Mara is the player's character: he walks every room, crouches, lies down, talks (the rig animates this one drawing).
In the game 232 art pixels tall (116 world pixels; people are built on a 1-art-pixel grid with the master palette, so the face is only a few dozen art pixels): design for that size, with a clear silhouette, a distinctive head and colours that tell them apart from everyone else in the cast.
Mara at the start of a new adventure: he wakes in his tent in everyday clothes and gears up (armour, the gauntlet) when he takes the ember cell from its cradle. Identical face, hair, skin, build and height to char-mara, so the player knows him at once.
