# Pilot pack

A small style test before the full set: one room, Mara, one object, one item, one card and the fight enemy. Bring them in with `npm run art:import`, look at them in the game, and adjust art/STYLE.md if they do not sit together.

- [room-mara](room-mara.md): The kiln hall: a vast vaulted hall built inside an old kiln, the glowing kiln mouths across the back, a pulley hoist (left), storage jars, an ash cart, the curtain to the firing house (right), and the spot where Mara's tent stands.
- [rack](rack.md): The charging rack in the tent: a lantern-frame cradle that holds the gauntlet's ember cell (full: the cell glowing in it; empty).
- [char-mara](char-mara.md): Mara, the courier and kiln-minder (he/him): ochre scarf and cloak, ceramic-plated armour, a satchel, and his mother's pressure gauntlet on his right arm (the ember cell unlit in this sheet).
- [battle-scavenger](battle-scavenger.md): The Hatch Scavenger: a squat pressure-machine with ceramic claws and bucket arms, glowing furnace belly (it climbs out of a floor hatch in the room, and stands opposite Mara in the fight).
- [card-strike](card-strike.md): Knuckle Drive: a gauntleted fist driving forward in a burst of sparks.
- [item-gauntlet](item-gauntlet.md): Mara's pressure gauntlet.
