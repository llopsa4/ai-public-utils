Authoring reference; may contain chapter spoilers. Runtime implementation is separate.

- Story and puzzle intent live in the linked STORY.md scenes; this specification owns visual facts, target classification, source references, placements, stages and attachments. Generated references are never edited directly.

- Finished-art planning only: runtime logic now exists in src/chapters/kofi1 with separate simple placeholders. Reference regeneration does not import assets, install either Kofi rig or generate finished game art.

- The six locations appear in story order. The woodland clearing contains timber work, tree huts and watch shelter; ground huts, gardens and the rite remain on the riverbank.

- Exactly one of Enali and Deyu survives after the explicit rescue. Enali route assembles the trough; Deyu route begins with assembled trough and fitted screen. All common stages retain the chosen human survivor.

- Each human-survivor route supports all four kill/release combinations for last wolf and bear: eight complete narrative paths. No corpse, trophy or extra resource reward is commissioned.

- A source pickup, carried item and installed representation are one identity. Hide the old representation when it moves. Dedicated later supplies are not resurrected Tsalohi objects.

- Structural geometry and foreground cuts have no automatic Look action. Targets are the existing story-defined inspections and puzzle uses; actor dialogue targets remain character staging references.

- Alternate state drawings share dimensions and anchors. Hide means no empty PNG. Semantic-only changes reuse existing drawings. Inventory icons are 64x64 and include only carried forms.

- World coordinates use 1280x720 art pixels; divide by two for logical units. Keep the approved palette and two-pixel world grid. Runtime controls text and lighting; moonlight is steady.

- Use labelled stage controls for illustrative snapshots; All overlays shows alternative footprints, not a simultaneous scene. Per-action visibility conditions remain in each asset and placement record.

- The hut support wedge remains installed under the beam at the ending. Only the loose pole returns beside the hearth.

- The 224-art-pixel Kofi scale is a planning reference only. Existing character/animal designs and poses are excluded from this pack; no new portraits, combat images or cards.

- The original pre-alignment pack is preserved in before-alignment.zip for recovery only, not as an editable authority.

- Deyu made Kofi’s original folding bow. Basic hunting arrows, a plain wooden blunt arrow and a light-cord arrow exist in the prologue; later engineered forked/weighted/cutting heads belong to Nessa’s rebuild.
