## Description

Silas, the con man (he/him), about 30, a bit mad and very eccentric: medium height (a little shorter than Mara), slim and loose-limbed, a performer's posture, restless hands. Pale olive skin, a narrow clever face, sharp cheekbones, quick dark eyes that are a touch too bright, a wide lopsided grin always halfway to a joke, short black hair sticking up in odd tufts after a night in a cell, a few days' stubble.
