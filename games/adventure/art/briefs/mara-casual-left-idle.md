## Description

Mara, the courier (he/him), about 22: the tallest of the cast but lean and long-limbed, a runner's build. Warm brown skin, short dark-brown hair swept back and a little unruly, a light stubble, hazel eyes, a determined, slightly tired face. An ochre wool scarf wrapped twice round his neck, its long end thrown over his left shoulder like a short cloak. Cream-white fired-ceramic armour plates (chest, shoulders, forearms, shins), chipped and patched, laced over dark brown leather. A worn leather satchel on his left hip. Dark work trousers with ceramic knee plates, patched at the knees; brown leather courier boots with brass buckles. His mother's pressure gauntlet on his right forearm and hand, oversized: a clenched fist of massive cream-ceramic knuckles, thick riveted brass reinforcements, a broad cuff and a pressure pipe along the forearm, the fist reaching down towards his knee; a round ember-cell housing on the back of the hand (unlit: dull dark red glass). Keep to Codex's pilot design (art/inbox/reference/gauntlet-closed-v2/mara-master.png). In his casual clothes (exactly the outfit of this pack's right-facing master.png), now drawn FACING LEFT. The left-facing standing picture when still: turned a little more towards us (a slight three-quarter view facing left), both eyes visible, the same relaxed pose as the right-facing idle.png of this outfit. No gauntlet, armour or satchel in this outfit; it is nearly symmetric, but draw it facing left all the same (light, hair and folds as seen from his left side). The thin leather cord bracelet is on his RIGHT wrist, so facing left it is on the FAR hand; the scarf hangs loose round his neck.
