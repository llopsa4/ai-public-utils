## Description

Mara, the courier (he/him), about 22: the tallest of the cast but lean and long-limbed, a runner's build. Warm brown skin, short dark-brown hair swept back and a little unruly, a light stubble, hazel eyes, a determined, slightly tired face. An ochre wool scarf wrapped twice round his neck, its long end thrown over his left shoulder like a short cloak. Cream-white fired-ceramic armour plates (chest, shoulders, forearms, shins), chipped and patched, laced over dark brown leather. A worn leather satchel on his left hip. Dark work trousers with ceramic knee plates, patched at the knees; brown leather courier boots with brass buckles. His mother's pressure gauntlet on his right forearm and hand, oversized: a clenched fist of massive cream-ceramic knuckles, thick riveted brass reinforcements, a broad cuff and a pressure pipe along the forearm, the fist reaching down towards his knee; a round ember-cell housing on the back of the hand (unlit: dull dark red glass). Keep to Codex's pilot design (art/inbox/reference/gauntlet-closed-v2/mara-master.png).

## Use and placement

Mara is the player's character: he walks every room, crouches, lies down, talks (the rig animates this one drawing).
In the game 232 art pixels tall (116 world pixels; people are built on a 1-art-pixel grid with the master palette, so the face is only a few dozen art pixels): design for that size, with a clear silhouette, a distinctive head and colours that tell them apart from everyone else in the cast.
