# Survival art pipeline

How final art replaces Claude's code-drawn placeholders, one asset at a time. Sizes, anchors and
the manifest format are in [SPEC.md](SPEC.md); request wording is in
[PROMPTING.md](../PROMPTING.md) (ART stage); agent ownership and handoffs are in the shared
[workflow](../../WORKFLOW.md). Art starts after the slice playtest ([PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) Phase D).

## Steps

1. **Claude writes the prompt** for one asset (template below) and adds it as a numbered request in
   [handoff/from-claude.md](../handoff/from-claude.md) *Requests for Codex*, with the asset key, the
   SPEC.md row, output path and **Approval authority: user**. While Codex is paused
   ([WORKLOG.md](../WORKLOG.md)) the request waits.
2. **Codex generates** the image, removes the background, crops to the SPEC.md canvas with the
   anchor in place, builds frame strips, and delivers `art/inbox/<key>/` (PNG + a note of the prompt
   used) in its handoff file. If the prompt cannot be followed, Codex says why instead of improvising.
3. **The user approves** the delivery (the first sprite of each kind sets the look for the rest).
4. **Install** (Codex, or Claude while Codex is paused, except generation): move the PNG to
   `art/<key>.png`, set the manifest entry to `final`, add the row in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md), and
   check it in the game: right size against a 64 x 32 tile, feet on the ground, correct depth,
   no console errors.

## Prompt template

```text
Hand-painted isometric game sprite in the style of a classic 2D city-builder (Stronghold-like),
warm natural colours, soft shading, light from the upper left, shadow to the lower right.
Subject: <one subject from its design>.
View: isometric 2:1 angle, standing on a 64x32 ground diamond, fully inside the frame with an empty
margin.
Background: transparent (or one flat pure #00FF00 to key out; no green in the subject).
No text, no border, no extra objects.
```

- Multi-frame sprites: one prompt per pose, same subject wording, so frames match; Codex assembles
  the strip.
- Ground tiles: a seamless diamond that tiles with its neighbours.
- Real-culture detail comes only from a CULTURE.md entry; otherwise it is plainly invented.
  Claude's limits ([PROMPTING.md](../PROMPTING.md) *Standing rules*) apply to every prompt.

## Checks before asking for approval

- Clean alpha, no fringe on dark or light ground; canvas and anchor exactly as SPEC.md.
- Reads at game size (a person at 32 px) by silhouette and colour.
- Matches the approved sprites of its kind (light, palette, angle).
- Free to use: generated by Codex or made with free software; no asset-store art.
