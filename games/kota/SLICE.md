# Kota first slice — spec (APPROVED by the user, 2026-11-18)

Task T3, written by Claude (Opus), 2026-11-18. Follows [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) and
[CULTURE.md](CULTURE.md). Detailed sources are in [research/dakota.md](research/dakota.md) (cited
as **RD**). Status tags: **✔** approved in CULTURE.md · **⏳ NR n** waits on *Needs review* item n ·
**🎮** game invention (no cultural claim). The user approved the spec with all its ⏳ and 🎮 items on 2026-11-18.

## Scope

One Dakota run: camp → 5 rooms → Iya boss → back to camp. Woman hero. Grey-box (placeholder
shapes) until art is approved. Phone landscape, touch controls as in DESIGN.md.

## Premise of the run

Iya, the Camp-Eater, came disguised as what the people most wanted and swallowed a camp (✔,
Zitkala-Ša via RD). The hero follows him through the homeland. In the story he is beaten at dawn,
when his thin legs fail him (RD), so the boss weakens as the light rises (🎮 mechanic built on the
story). When he is defeated, the swallowed camp walks out freed, which is the run's reward
(*released, not taken*).

## Home region (⏳ choice: Minnesota River Valley, Mdewakanton homeland)

Woods, lakes and rivers; maple-sugar groves; a wild-rice lake; prairie edges with sage and willow
(Lower Sioux, Shakopee and Eastman via RD). **Bdote and Bde Wakan are never arenas** (RD Restricted).

| Room | Place | Season cue |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lakeshore with reeds and mud | summer |
| 2 | River-valley woodland | summer |
| 3 | Maple-sugar grove (birch-bark troughs) | spring |
| 4 | Wild-rice lake edge | autumn |
| 5 | Prairie with sage and willow | night before dawn |
| Boss | Open prairie at dawn | dawn |

The room order is random from 1–5 and the boss is always last (🎮).

## Hero (woman; shared moveset)

- **Look:** a buckskin dress with short sleeves, decorated with beads and porcupine quills; moccasins
  (Eastman via RD). No feathers or regalia (✔ Off-limits).
- **Base attack:** a stone-axe combo, three hits (stone axe and short-handled axe in the stories; RD).
- **Dash:** a short roll, with brief invulnerability (🎮).
- **Ability 1, Arrow volley:** a fast bow burst. Eastman names the bow as the main weapon, plus a
  speed-shooting arrow game (RD).
- **Ability 2, Track:** marks nearby enemies and shows where they'll attack. Tracking is in Eastman's
  training and women read footprints (RD).
- No poisoned arrows (RD advises leaving them out).

## Threats (3 room enemies + boss)

- **Boss: Iya, the Camp-Eater** (✔). Phase 1 (dark): lunges and a swallow attack the hero escapes
  from. Phase 2 (dawn rising): slower, legs falter, so he's open to attack.
- **Room enemies, 🎮 abstract spirit threats** (DESIGN category: hunger, winter, sickness). They
  aren't drawn from any nation's story, so they're shapeless and use no cultural symbols:
  - **Frost wisp:** slows the hero; when beaten, the land renews (food pickup).
  - **Hunger shade:** drains healing; when beaten, the land renews.
  - **Blight swarm:** a group of small weak units; when beaten, the land renews.
- **Not used:** animals (relatives), eagles, Unktehi, Inyan, Thunder beings, Double-Face (⏳ NR 3).

## Rewards

- **Gifts, 1 of 3 after each room** (⏳ NR 5, helpers from Zitkala-Ša via RD):
  - **Skiska the duck:** a warning, so the dash recharges when an enemy winds up.
  - **Patkasa the turtle:** a shell, so the first hit in each room is blocked.
  - **The muskrat:** faster movement.
  - **The coyote:** rolling through hazards clears them.
  - **Manstin the rabbit:** more arrow-volley damage.
  - **The youngest bear:** a heal after each room (it shared food).
- **Land renews:** food pickups only (wild rice, maple sugar, dried meat; Eastman via RD). No
  medicines until ⏳ NR 8 is decided.
- **Iya defeated:** the camp walks out freed. Gives camp progress and one item from the table above.

## Camp (2 unlocks)

- **Maple-sugar grove:** more healing from food (Eastman via RD).
- **Practice ground** (archery game, lacrosse): an arrow-volley upgrade (Eastman's games via RD;
  ⏳ the lacrosse depiction needs a check, because it isn't ceremonial in RD but is culturally
  significant).
- **Elder telling:** after the first Iya encounter, an elder tells the Camp-Eater story (WRITE task
  later, from Zitkala-Ša only).
