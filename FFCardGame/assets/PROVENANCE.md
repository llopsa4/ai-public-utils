## Opening chapter illustrations — 2026-10-31

Generated with the built-in OpenAI `image_gen` tool. The tool did not expose a model identifier. Five cutouts preserve the generated RGBA transparency; environments and square card tiles are RGB. Approved sources were copied into `assets/opening/` without resampling or pixel edits. Card and sprite proportions/crops are controlled in CSS. Fitted Brace and Return Stroke were refined with the same built-in tool to remove incidental character faces; the accepted v2 filenames are used at runtime. Their initial variants are kept only in ignored review files.

People are consumed by ScenePlayer and Iven’s support portrait. Machines are consumed by Battlefield (including tutorial/training variants). Environment plates appear in scenes, chapter maps, the workshop and combat. Card tiles appear in reward choices and the hand. Runtime names, costs and rules are HTML, never baked into the images.
